🚀 Featured Projects
🍱 Food Bridge Connect
Food Bridge Connect is an Android application focused on reducing food waste by connecting surplus food with people or organizations that can make use of it.
The project explores features such as surplus food prediction, freshness estimation, smart matching, geolocation, route optimization, and ratings, with food-safety considerations based on FSSAI guidelines. Developed collaboratively using Kotlin and Android Studio.
Tech: `Kotlin` `Android` `Firebase` `AI/ML` `Geolocation`

🏛️ Delhi Heritage Explorer
Delhi Heritage Explorer was developed as part of the Dilli AI Grind project ecosystem, with the goal of making Delhi's historical and cultural locations easier to discover through a digital experience.
The project involved exploring how technology can be used to organize heritage information and create a more engaging way to explore important places across Delhi.
